NLP TOPIC KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================

CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION TO NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING
chapter_id: CH01
part: Introduction
keywords: NLP, NLU, text-based, dialog-based, evaluation
summary:
NLP studies computational methods for processing natural language.
The course introduces applications, levels of language analysis,
representation and understanding, and organization of natural language
understanding systems.

subtopics:
1.1 The study of natural language
1.2 Applications of Natural Language Understanding
1.2.1 Text-based applications
1.2.2 Dialog-based applications
1.3 Evaluating Natural Language Understanding Systems
1.4 Different levels of language analysis
1.5 Representation and understanding
1.6 Organization of NLU systems
